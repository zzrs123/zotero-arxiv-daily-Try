- Article: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15031v1

Level leakage constitutes a dominant error source in quantum state control and necessitates accurate characterization for large-scale quantum computing. Quantum sensing provides a viable route for high-precision estimation of level leakage, but its sensitivity can be limited by non-unitary leakage dynamics and interference between the free Hamiltonian and signal generator. This work proposes a reinforcement learning-based quantum sensing algorithm for Hermitian three-level and effective dissipative two-level systems. The algorithm adopts an experimentally feasible pulse-parameter modulation strategy, enabling high-precision and reliable measurement under the specified control and measurement protocol. In both models, DQN-discovered pulse sequences enhance the measurement-model-specific sensing precision, while joint phase--detuning modulation further improves the performance and yields a CFI close to the corresponding QFI.

## 2. A Field-Deployable 2U Ultra-Stable Laser with Sub-Hertz Linewidth Based on Optoelectronic Oscillator Locking

- Authors: Dongdong Jiao, Linbo Zhang, Xue Deng, Jing Gao, Yue Gao, Mengfan Wu, Qi Zang, Guanjun Xu, Tao Liu
- Source hits: arxiv
- Matched researchers: Yue Gao
- Matched groups: N/A

- Topic keywords: N/A
- Journal/source: arxiv
- Publication date: 2026-11-11
- Article: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15005v1

Compact, high-performance ultra-stable lasers that can operate outside the laboratory remain a long-standing goal. Here we report, to our knowledge, the most compact field-deployable ultra-stable laser, packaged in a standard 2U rack chassis. It is stabilized to a 50 mm-long, 19 mL optical reference cavity with low vibration sensitivity by optoelectronic oscillator (OEO) locking, an approach particularly suited to compact, transportable systems. The laser delivers an integrated linewidth of about 0.42 Hz and a fractional frequency stability of about 5E-15 at 1 s averaging time (about 2.2E-15 at 0.4 s). Measured phase noise approaches the cavity thermal-noise limit below 100 Hz and the theoretical OEO-locking limit from 100 Hz to 100 kHz. The system volume is roughly one quarter of the volume of previously reported integrated, transportable ultra-stable lasers of comparable performance, making it well suited for field applications in time-frequency metrology, quantum technologies, and environmental sensing.
